## 1. What the Bot does

The Bot is a free role-playing game played with slash commands and buttons on Discord, in servers where it has been added. Support tickets (/support) are followed on the official server or in direct messages with the Bot. You create a hero, explore, fight, craft, trade with other players, join guilds and take part in shared events.

On the official Aldoria server, the Bot also:
- gives roles that reflect your progress in the game (class, rank, language);
- publishes the server's rules, announcements and game events, with a button to translate them;
- creates suggestion and bug report posts from the forms you fill in;
- opens private support tickets between you and the team;
- welcomes new members;
- posts leaderboards updated live, including the vote leaderboard.
