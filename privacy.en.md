# Privacy Policy: Aldoria

*Français : [privacy.md](./privacy.md)*

**Last updated: 4 October 2026**

This policy explains what information the Discord bot **Aldoria** (the "Bot") processes, why, for how long, and what your rights are. It complements the [Terms of Service](./terms.en.md).

The Bot is developed and operated by its creator (the "Operator"), who is the controller of the data described here.

## 1. Scope

The Bot is a role-playing game played through Discord commands and buttons, in the servers where it has been added. Support tickets (/support) can also be followed in direct messages with the Bot. On its official Discord server, it also manages roles, posts, support tickets, welcome messages, leaderboards and announcements.

## 2. Data processed

**Identification**: your Discord user ID, your username and display name, the language of your Discord client, and, on the official server, the language role you hold.

**Your game data**: your hero (name, class, level, experience, coins, items, equipment, progress, quests, achievements, guild, house, pets...), your game settings (language, private mode, notification preferences), and the times of your last actions needed by the game (daily reward, energy, travels...).

**Interactions between players**: in-game mail, trades, market orders and sales, bets, duels, guild membership and contributions. Other players see what the game shows them (your hero's name, rankings, guild, market offers).

**Servers where you play**: the ID and name of the Discord servers where you use the Bot, for the server leaderboard.

**Activity log**: to understand problems, spot abuse and follow the life of the game, the Bot records every command, button, choice and form you use: your user ID and username, your hero's name and level, the server and channel (or "direct message"), the options chosen, the processing time and the outcome (success, error, refusal). Values typed in the game's forms are recorded in shortened form. Auction house listings and purchases are recorded too (item, quantity, price, buyer, seller).

**Adding the Bot to a server**: when the Bot joins a server, it records the server's public information (name, ID, creation date, member and online counts, boosts, number of channels and roles), its owner and the person who added the Bot (user ID, username, account creation date), the permissions given to the Bot, and it compares the member list with our sanction lists to flag members already banned from the game or from the official server. The member list itself is not kept: only flagged members and the counts are.

**Votes**: if you vote for the Bot on top.gg or discordbotlist.com, these sites send us your Discord user ID. We keep the date and site of each vote to give you your reward, enforce the delay between two votes and keep the vote leaderboard.

**Anti-cheat**: to detect bots and macros, the Bot records how you play: the number of actions per hour (last 72 hours), the time between your last 80 actions, and how quickly you claim your daily reward. It computes a suspicion score from this. These records contain no message content.

**Official server**:
- the roles the Bot gives you (class, rank, language, Hero), and the language you choose with the Translate menu;
- the suggestion and bug report forms you send, with their screenshots: they are published as public posts on the server;
- support tickets: the content of the form, the messages you write in your ticket, their edits and deletions, and the files you send there. They are copied into a forum reserved for the team, along with internal notes written by the team, the team member in charge, response and closing times, and an HTML transcript generated when the ticket is closed;
- your arrival and departure: user ID, username, account creation date, and your standing in the game (hero, ban);
- sanctions taken on the official server (ban, timeout), with their author and reason read from Discord's audit log;
- your hero's name and level in the leaderboards posted live, and your mention in the vote leaderboard.

**Message content**: the Bot has the "Message Content" permission only to relay the messages of support tickets between you and the team. It processes only messages written in ticket threads, in direct messages for a ticket, and in the team's ticket forum. It also deletes, without reading or keeping them, replies written under the suggestion and bug panels. It does not read, store or analyse any other conversation.

**Blacklist**: if a server or a person is blacklisted, we keep its ID, its name at the time, the reason and the date. The Bot then leaves that server, or the servers that person owns or added the Bot to.

**Technical logs**: errors and warnings, which may include your user ID or the ID of a server, used only to fix problems.

**What the Bot does not process**: no payment data (the Bot sells nothing), no email address, no phone number, no location. No data is collected for advertising, profiling or resale.

## 3. Purposes and legal bases

| Purpose | Legal basis |
|---|---|
| Run the game: your hero, progress, rankings, trades, guilds, events | Performance of the contract (the Terms) |
| Send you the game notifications you enabled, by direct message | Your choice in the game settings, which you can change at any time |
| Detect cheating and keep the game fair | Legitimate interest |
| Activity log, server reports, blacklist | Legitimate interest in securing the service and preventing abuse |
| Reward votes and keep their leaderboard | Your choice to vote, and performance of the contract |
| Official server: roles, translations, posts, welcome | Legitimate interest in running the community |
| Support tickets, moderation, appeals | Performance of the contract and legitimate interest |
| Technical logs and backups | Legitimate interest in keeping the service working |

## 4. Retention

- **Game data**: kept while your hero exists. You can ask for its deletion at any time (see section 7).
- **Anti-cheat**: actions per hour for 72 hours, the last 80 action intervals, the last 7 daily reward claims, and the latest suspicion score.
- **Tickets, messages and transcripts**: kept as long as the service runs, as a record of past requests and decisions. You may ask for their deletion.
- **Traces of the team's actions**: 180 days, then erased automatically.
- **Your ticket thread**: locked and removed from your list 24 hours after closing.
- **Activity log and technical logs**: files kept for 14 days on the Bot's machine. A readable copy is posted in private channels of the official server, restricted to the Operator; you may ask for what concerns you to be deleted.
- **Server and sanction reports**: kept in the same private channels, as long as the service runs.
- **Votes**: date and site of each vote, kept for 12 months for the leaderboard and rewards.
- **Blacklist**: until removed from the list.
- **Backups of the ticket database**: daily copies kept for 14 days.
- **Backups of the game database**: one copy a day kept for 14 days, and one a week kept for 8 weeks. Deleted data therefore leaves the backups within 8 weeks at most.
- **Ban**: if you are banned, the ban record (user ID, reason, date) is kept so that it can be enforced.

## 5. Who can access your data

- **Other players**: only what the game displays (hero name, rankings, guild, market offers, public posts on the official server).
- **The Aldoria team**: support tickets and moderation information, in channels reserved for the team.
- **Discord Inc.**, which transmits and hosts messages under its own [privacy policy](https://discord.com/privacy).
- **top.gg and discordbotlist.com**, only if you vote there: voting happens on their site, under their own privacy rules; they send us your Discord user ID. We send them the Bot's server count, never any data about you.
- **The Bot's technical host**, which runs the program and its database, only as far as necessary.

Your data is never sold, rented, or passed to any other third party.

## 6. Team anonymity

In support tickets, the team's answers reach you without a name, signed "ALDORIA · TEAM". Only you and the Bot are in your ticket's thread.

## 7. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object to and port your data. You can turn off direct message notifications at any time in the game settings.

To exercise your rights, open a ticket on the official server ("Help and questions"). You will get an answer within one month. Deleting your data deletes your hero and all your progress permanently.

Erasure may be refused or delayed for what is needed to enforce a ban or to meet a legal obligation. If you believe your rights are not respected, you may lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the [CNIL](https://www.cnil.fr), or with your own national authority.

## 8. Security

The database is stored on the Bot's host with access limited to the Operator, and backed up every day; copies may be kept on a second private medium of the Operator to survive a failure. Team channels and tickets are restricted to the people who need them, and the owner's web dashboard requires a Discord login and a two-factor code. As no system is infallible, absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

## 9. Minors

The Bot is not aimed specifically at minors and knowingly collects nothing beyond what is described here. Using Discord is subject to the minimum age set by Discord's own terms.

## 10. Changes

This policy may change as the Bot evolves. The date at the top of the document is then updated; for any significant change, an announcement is posted on the official server.
